About SVCE

Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering (SVCE) is a premier engineering institution located in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Established in 1985, SVCE has been at the forefront of technical education, fostering innovation, research, and holistic development. The college is approved by AICTE and affiliated to Anna University, offering undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various engineering disciplines.


The Speakers' Forum, SVCE's official debating society, has been organizing Model United Nations conferences to provide students with a platform to develop diplomacy skills, critical thinking, and global awareness. SVCEMUN'26 continues this rich tradition of academic excellence and student engagement.
